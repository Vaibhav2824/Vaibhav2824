### 👨‍💻 About me

- 🎓 Final-year **B.Tech CSE (AI & ML)** at PES University, Bengaluru
- 🎙️ Ex-**Data Science Intern at Rezo.ai**, working on voice AI for banking and finance contact centres. I shipped a classifier to production and benchmarked 9 speech-to-text models across 6 Indian languages
- 🔍 My favourite bug so far: a speech benchmark that looked great only because the test set had leaked. The honest rerun found an engine at 14.9% error vs the incumbent's 26.9%
